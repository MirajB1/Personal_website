I'm an Associate Machine Learning Engineer at Techkey Technology Pvt. Ltd. in Kathmandu — building, training and fine-tuning models in Python, benchmarking them against their baselines, and turning the ones that work into code the team can maintain. AgniDrishti Nepal

AI wildfire prediction & early warning system for Nepal

capstone project live

problem
Wildfires spread across Nepal faster than anyone can report them, and the people at risk find out last.
approach
Custom-trained Random Forest and Naive Bayes models scoring wildfire risk from weather, elevation and satellite fire-hotspot data — with Vapour Pressure Deficit computed as an engineered feature.
build
A JWT-secured FastAPI backend with role-based access for admins and the public, live incident reporting, automated full-country risk scans, and a React front end with real-time hotspot mapping.

NASA FIRMS · OpenWeatherMap · Open-Elevation

Project Ecolog

Forest fire & intrusion detection on a Raspberry Pi

winner · Tech Conclave 2024technical category · BPC Hackfest 2024

problem
A forest can't tell you it's burning, and by the time smoke is visible from a road it's already late.
approach
Put ears and eyes in the trees: real-time audio classification for gunshots and smoke, paired with computer vision on the same device.
build
Raspberry Pi running the classifiers, Azure Custom Vision for smoke and fire imagery, Azure Blob Storage archiving frames for later analysis. Triggers hardware alerts and streams live data to a web dashboard.
team
Four people. Two wins.
